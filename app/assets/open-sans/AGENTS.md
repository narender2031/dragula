# open-sans

**Parent**: [assets](../AGENTS.md) | **Repository**: A simple toolbar to drag and drop images from Unsplash to anywhere.

> Contains a collection of TrueType Font files for the Open Sans typeface, providing various weights and styles for text rendering. Provides the necessary typographic data for displaying text in bold, italic, extra bold, and light variations of the Open Sans font.
